Before touching a single cell, this is what's needed.

The file import_Aston_Bocettos_City_Suites.xlsx . It's not a document to read: it's a document to edit.

It contains 7 sheets — Access Point, Cabling, Rooms, Software, Infrastructure, Workstation and IT MPL — with the proposed nomenclature for each item to supervise its progress during the property's preopening.

Based on the provided blueprints, these locations have been extracted and the proposed nomenclature has been created for the items of each category.

What we ask is simple: go through it, update it with your reality, and return it. If your names, your states or your columns are different from ours, please send it to us and we'll map it here.

If there are extra places or items, this is the mechanism to synchronize on-site work with progress supervision so we all speak the same language.

The Excel travels with you Sent attached · Returned edited · Synced with the app
The journey

Four steps, one single flow.

01

Review

Open the Excel. Each sheet is a chapter: hardware, cabling, rooms, software, infrastructure, workstations and purchases.

02

Align

Compare our names (F03-Room-301) with yours. If they differ, send them to us so we can map them and give them proper tracking.

03

Update

Change FALSE to TRUE where it's already done. Fill in serial numbers, IPs, photos and dates. Add rows if your property has more equipment or items, and remove the ones that no longer apply.

04

Return

Send us your on-site survey on these topics so we can map and update your nomenclatures in our pre-opening control. We'll sync the app with your real structure on-site.

Why updating matters

Every item counts. Every TRUE measures progress.

Each row of the Excel represents a real physical item in the property: an AP, a camera, a TV, a door lock, a tablet, a switch. Preopening progress is measured item by item, not by blocks or general or partial estimates — accuracy is required.

That's why it's key that the team goes through the Excel and punctually marks each completed task: FALSE → TRUE where it's already done, and fills in serial numbers, IPs, photos and observations in the open columns. This way the preopening app reflects the real state of the property and we can measure how much has been advanced day by day, minute by minute, by category and by department.

Any adjustment, question or feedback please send it to us by email.

Access Points (APs)
Sheet 01 · 137 records

The APs that give life to WiFi.

Each row is an Access Point. Progress is counted in four steps: mounting, firmware, configuration and connection.

✓Mounted: the AP is physically in place.
✓Firmware Updated: with the latest version.
✓Configured: SSID, VLAN and controller ready.
✓Online: visible and responding.

Core Switches
Category · Network Infrastructure

The brain of the entire network.

Core switches (Core Switch 1 and 2) with SFP uplinks to the distribution. They are configured before distribution switches.

✓Installed: core switch mounted in rack.
✓Configured: layer 3, VLANs and routes.

✓WAN1: primary ISP router operational.

Cabling
Sheet 02 · ~400 records

The cabling that nobody sees, but holds everything.

From SS to N35, from F01 to F17, from lifts to shafts. Each network point is validated in four steps until certified.

✓Pulled: cable pulled to the point.
✓Terminated: connected at patch panel or faceplate.
✓Labeled: labeled at both ends.
✓Tested: certified with tester.
