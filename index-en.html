Before touching a single cell, here is what is needed.

The file import_Aston_Bocettos_City_Suites.xlsx . It is not a document to read: it is a document to edit.

It contains 7 sheets — Access Point, Cabling, Rooms, Software, Infrastructure, Workstation, and IT MPL — with the proposed nomenclature for each item to supervise its progress during the property's preopening.

Based on the drawings provided, these locations have been extracted and the proposed nomenclature has been created for the items in each category.

What we ask of you is simple: go through it, update it with your reality, and return it. If your names, your statuses, or your columns are different from ours, please send it to us and we will map it here.

If there are extra places or items, this is the mechanism to synchronize the work on site with the progress supervision so everyone speaks the same language.

The Excel travels with you Sent as attachment · Returned edited · Synced with the app
The journey

Four steps, a single flow.

01

Review

Open the Excel. Each sheet is a chapter: hardware, cabling, rooms, software, infrastructure, workstations, and purchases.

02

Align

Compare our names (F03-Room-301) with yours. If they differ, send it to us so we can map it and give it proper follow-up.

03

Update

Change FALSE to TRUE where it is done. Fill in serials, IPs, photos, and dates. Add rows if your property has more equipment or items, and delete those that are no longer present.

04

Return

Send us your on-site field survey on these topics so we can map and update your nomenclatures in our pre-opening control. We will sync the app with your real on-site structure.

Why updating matters

Every item counts. Every TRUE measures progress.

Each row in the Excel represents a real physical item on the property: an AP, a camera, a TV, a lock, a tablet, a switch. Preopening progress is measured item by item, not by blocks or general or partial estimates; exactness is required.

That is why it is key that the team goes through the Excel and marks each completed task accurately: FALSE → TRUE where it is done, and fills in serials, IPs, photos, and observations in the open columns. This way the preopening app reflects the real status of the property and we can measure how much has been advanced day by day, minute by minute, by category and by department.

Any adjustment, question, or feedback please send it to us by email.
